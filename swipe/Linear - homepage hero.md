## Why it works

Linear names its own market frame, a "product development system", instead of competing as another issue tracker or project-management tool. That frame sets buyers' expectations (built for product teams, not general project tracking) before they see a feature. "Purpose-built" and "teams and agents" signal who it's for and why it's different now, in plain words.

## Copy

- **Headline:** "The product development system for teams and agents"
- **Subhead:** "Purpose-built for planning and building products. Designed for the AI era."
- **Section opener:** "A new species of product tool."
- **Structure:** headline and subhead, then a live-looking product screenshot in which an agent picks up an issue, then logos, then three short proof pillars (purpose-built, powered by agents, designed for speed).
