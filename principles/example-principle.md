# Fix positioning before messaging

**The principle:** When a launch, website or pitch isn't landing, check the positioning before rewriting the copy. New words on weak positioning just make the same problem sound different.

**Why I hold it:** Messaging expresses decisions that positioning makes: who we beat, what only we do, who cares most, which market we're in. If those decisions are vague, no headline will fix it, and sales ends up correcting the wrong assumptions buyers bring in from the category we've named ([[A Quickstart Guide to Positioning - April Dunford|April Dunford]]). The same holds for the sales story: a narrative only works once the context is set ([[The Greatest Sales Deck I've Ever Seen - Andy Raskin|Andy Raskin]]).

**How I apply it:**
- Before any messaging brief, ask: can we name the alternative we beat, and the segment that cares most?
- If the answer is no, run a positioning session first, even a short one.
- If the answer is yes, messaging work is the right job: go ahead.

**When it doesn't apply:** A quick fix to one page or ad when the positioning is already agreed and working in deals.
